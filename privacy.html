Privacy policy

Dastyar Bot is a private tool for one team. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What it accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages when a team member asks, and sends email only after an administrator approves it in Telegram.
  • Google Drive: only files and the folder the bot itself created ("Dastyar Documents"), to archive team documents. It cannot see other files.

How it is used

Data is used only to answer the team's own requests. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or to train models. Email content is processed by an AI model (Anthropic) solely to answer the request that triggered it.

Storage and deletion

Documents are kept on the team's server and in the Drive folder. Access tokens are stored on the server and never shown. To stop access, revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions; to delete data, delete the Drive folder or ask the administrator.
